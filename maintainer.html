Why this project

The same job, on a substrate nobody governs yet.

Context Stack is not a change of field. It is the discipline of contractual accountability applied to systems that currently have none.

Enterprise delivery has spent decades building the machinery of accountability: service levels with teeth, incident discipline, change control, audit trails, evidence that survives a review. Agentic AI is being deployed into those same estates with almost none of it — and with a common assumption that the model can be asked to police itself.

Context Stack takes the opposite position. Enforcement lives outside the model. Policy is compiled, decisions are reproducible, and every decision is bound to a hash of the policy that produced it. The framework is an attempt to give AI actions the same evidentiary standard that enterprise change management has required for twenty years.

The project is also built the way it argues systems should be built. Work is split across separate seats with enforced separation of duties: one specifies but never commits, one implements and is instructed that where the specification and the source disagree the source wins, and a single human ratifies anything irreversible. The governing protocol is in version control. Amendments record what is given up, not only what is gained. A published error ledger tracks every specification error and what caught it — the same standard of evidence the framework asks of anyone else.
